EMBODIED INTELLIGENCE & ROBOT LEARNING

Learning to perceive,
reason, and act.

I study how robots learn from multimodal observations and interaction to act in the physical world.

I am a master’s student in Electronic Information at UCAS and the Institute of Software, Chinese Academy of Sciences. My research interests span embodied reinforcement learning, vision-language-action models, and simulation for real-world robot learning.

Previously, I received an MSc in Data Science with Distinction from the University of Bristol and a B.Eng. in Artificial Intelligence from Chongqing University. I also work on intelligent software development, with a focus on automated code review and repair.

RealTwin-RL

Bridging Digital Twin Reinforcement Learning to Real-World VLA

Jiadong Xu, Yiwen Wang, Xiaoqi Li, Zhengyong Zhu, Zhanyang Gu, Hongwei Fan, Quanxin Hu, Haomin Wu, Zhanying Shao, Ran Cheng, Jiafeng Xu, Li Yang, Hao Dong

A Real2Sim2Real framework for VLA reinforcement learning post-training. Semantic milestones provide dense task-progress rewards, while a milestone-conditioned reality discriminator calibrates rewards to reduce exploitation of simulation artifacts.
